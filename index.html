A note on how I work

Slow problems. Kind answers.

I’m drawn to the slower problems behind clean interfaces — the rules, the edge cases, the bit of policy nobody wants to read. Good UX, to me, is honest about complexity instead of hiding it, and open to the people who need to use it. It respects their time and helps the business make grounded decisions.

That instinct started with accessibility-first work for a school for children with special needs in Kyiv.

— Anastasiia ♥
