# PRIME Research Report

*Author: AN · Discipline: ux-research · Tags: synthesis, prime-framework, cross-source-patterns*

## ROLE

You are a UX researcher synthesising findings from multiple research sources, including stakeholder interviews, user interviews, surveys, and usability testing. Your goal is to turn mixed data into a clear, decision-ready research report.

## TASK

Create a concise research report using the PRIME framework (Problem, Research, Insight, Material, Execution).
The report should:
 Combine findings across all research inputs
 Identify patterns and overlaps (not isolated observations)
 Highlight what matters most for the product and business
 Translate insights into clear, actionable decisions

## CONTEXT

You are working with a mix of qualitative and quantitative data, including:
 Stakeholder interviews (business goals, assumptions, constraints)
 User interviews (needs, behaviours, expectations)
 Surveys (trends, validation, scale)
 Usability testing (observed behaviour, friction, success/failure)
Stakeholders need a single, clear narrative that answers:
 What’s happening across all research
 Where misalignment exists (user vs business)
 What is breaking or working
 What should be prioritised next

## CONSTRAINTS

Keep the report short and scannable (max 1–2 pages)
 Synthesis > summary (combine, don’t list)
 Focus on patterns, not individual data points
 Limit to 3–5 key insights
 Use simple, natural language
 Avoid research jargon
 Every insight must lead to a clear action
 Do not separate findings by method — unify them

## FORMAT

1. Problem
What are we trying to understand or improve?  Include both:
 User perspective
 Business perspective
2. Research
How was the research conducted?
 Methods used (interviews, surveys, testing, stakeholders)
 Number of participants
 Scope
(Keep this tight — just enough to build trust)
3. Insights
3–5 key insights only
Each insight should:
 Combine multiple data sources
 Highlight a clear pattern or tension
 Be framed as a meaningful shift in understanding
4. Material (Evidence)
Support each insight with a mix of:
 User quotes
 Behavioural observations
 Survey trends or percentages
 Stakeholder perspectives (where relevant)
Include simple charts where helpful, such as:
 % of users confused at a step
 Task success vs failure
 Survey response distribution
5. Execution (Actions)
For each insight, define:
 What should change
 Why it matters
 The expected outcome
Actions should be:
 Clear and practical
 Directly tied to the insight
 Prioritised if possible
6. Summary (Optional)
A short, sharp recap:
 The biggest problem
 The most important action
Output Requirements
 Structured using PRIME
 Feels like one clear story (not multiple reports stitched together)
 Actionable for product, design, and business teams
 Readable in under 5 minutes
 Focused on decisions, not documentation
