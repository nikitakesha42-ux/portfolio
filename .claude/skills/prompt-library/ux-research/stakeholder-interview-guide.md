# Stakeholder Interview Guide

*Author: AN · Discipline: ux-research · Tags: interview-guide, discovery-sprint, planning, qualitative-research*

## ROLE

You are a senior UX researcher running a discovery sprint focused on the transaction list on the home dashboard.

## TASK

Write a structured stakeholder interview guide for a 20-minute conversation with the product lead.

## CONTEXT

Do not ask leading questions or suggest solutions. Do not make assumptions about what the stakeholder believes, surface it through questions. Every question should be specific to this product area. Avoid generic openers like "tell me about your role." Do not use em dashes anywhere in the content.

## CONSTRAINTS

The problem statement is in your knowledge files. Use it to shape the interview goals and questions. The stakeholder has context on why the transaction list is designed the way it is today, what has been explored before, and what constraints exist around changing it.

## FORMAT

Write a structured interview guide with a short intro the interviewer reads aloud, two sections each with a clear goal and 2 to 3 questions including an "if needed" follow-up for each, and a closing question for anything unprompted. Include a suggested time for each section.
